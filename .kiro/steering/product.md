# Pet Heaven

Pet Heaven is a fictional Singapore charity society for abandoned cats and dogs, created for the individual ISIT207 Assignment 3 coursework.

- Visitors browse and filter pets, save favourites, read adoption guidance and photo credits, and submit Release or Contact forms.
- Members join with one or more roles, including Adopter alone. They can edit their profile, apply for available pets, and track their own requests.
- Staff sign in through either login page. They review members and enquiries, filter records, record calls, update workflow status, keep private notes, export CSV, and manage staff-added pets and availability.
- Form submissions require an administrator notification and attempt an applicant acknowledgement. Donations and sponsorship are expressions of interest.
- Part A is the web app. Part B is a PDF review report and narrated screen recording with links. Attribute external material and label fictional content clearly.

## Assessment priorities

- Component-based React SPA, reusable components, validation, usability, and design quality.
- Extra-credit areas include deployment, searching and browsing, database integration, and integration tests.
