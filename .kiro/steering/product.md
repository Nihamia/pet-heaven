# Pet Heaven

Pet Heaven is a fictional Singapore charity society for abandoned cats and dogs. It is an individual ISIT207 Assignment 3 coursework project.

- Visitors browse, search, filter and favourite pets, read care and adoption guidance, view image credits, and send Release or Contact enquiries.
- Members register with one or more roles, including Adopter on its own. They sign in, edit their profile, apply for available pets, and track their own requests.
- Staff sign in through either login page. The dashboard covers members, adoption, release, messages, and staff-added pets. It supports role filters, follow-up and status changes, private notes, CSV, print, and pet publication and availability.
- Forms send an administrator notification and attempt an applicant acknowledgement. Donation and sponsorship are expressions of interest without payments.
- Keep the fiction and photo attributions clear. Part A is the app. Part B is the review report PDF and narrated recording with links.
